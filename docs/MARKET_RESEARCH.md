# Market Research: Mukalla & Yemeni Food Tech Landscape

## 1. Local Macro & Consumer Environment

Mukalla is the coastal economic and administrative capital of Hadhramaut, experiencing steady commercial growth, urbanization, and a high concentration of universities and colleges (such as Hadhramaut University and Al-Ahgaff University).

### Key Demographics & Habits:
1. **Smartphone Penetration**: High smartphone usage among youths, students, and working professionals. Android is dominant (~80%), followed by iOS (~20%).
2. **Connectivity**: 4G networks (Yemen Mobile, You, Sabafon) are prevalent, but connection speeds can vary. Lightweight web apps that cache assets and load under 2 seconds are critical.
3. **WhatsApp Culture**: WhatsApp is the de facto communication medium for commerce, customer service, and informal business inquiries.
4. **Food Delivery Realities**: Full delivery infrastructure in Mukalla suffers from unmapped street numbering and traffic congestion, making **Pickup / Takeaway** the fastest, most reliable, and most popular dining choice for busy evenings.

## 2. Payment Dynamics in Yemen

International payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay) are inaccessible in Yemen due to regional financial sanctions and banking disconnects. Instead, the local market relies on:
1. **Cash on Pickup**: Extremely common, highly trusted.
2. **Local Bank & Money Exchange Apps**:
   - **Kuraimi Islamic Microfinance Bank** (Haseb / Jawwal)
   - **Al-Amqi Exchange**
   - **Al-Busairi Exchange**
   - Mobile e-wallets (OneCash, Floosak, etc.)
3. **Receipt Screenshot Verification**: Customers transfer money via app, generate an official PDF or screenshot containing a transaction reference ID, and share it with the merchant. Merchants visually verify the receipt before handing over goods.

## 3. The Need for Direct Restaurant Ordering

While food aggregators (such as Safeer) exist, restaurants face significant downsides:
- Aggregator commissions reduce restaurant margins.
- Third-party drivers often cause delivery delays, and customer dissatisfaction is blamed on the restaurant's food quality.
- The restaurant does not capture customer data or build brand loyalty.
- Aggregators lack intelligent pick-up scheduling features.

A direct-to-consumer platform tailored to Pizza House gives the restaurant 100% control over its customer relationships, menus, and kitchen timing.
